
STUDENT HANDBOOK
Demonstration Policy Document
Academic Year: 2025-2027

SECTION 1: FEE PAYMENT POLICY

1. Students must pay their semester fees before the
official fee deadline communicated by the institution.

2. Students can check their fee payment status through
the student support system.

3. If a student experiences a technical failure in the
payment portal, the student should report the issue
to the accounts department with supporting evidence.

4. The accounts department will review the reported
technical issue and communicate the next steps.

5. A payment extension is not automatic when a
technical issue occurs. Students must obtain
confirmation from the accounts department.

SECTION 2: SCHOLARSHIP POLICY

1. Scholarship eligibility depends on the criteria
announced by the institution.

2. Students must submit the required scholarship
documents within the announced application period.

3. Scholarship approval is subject to verification
by the concerned department.

SECTION 3: COURSE CHANGE POLICY

1. Students requesting a course or elective change
must submit a formal request to the academic office.

2. Course changes are subject to seat availability
and academic approval.

3. Students should not assume that a course change
has been approved until confirmation is received.

SECTION 4: STUDENT SUPPORT POLICY

1. Students can raise support requests for academic,
administrative, technical and fee-related issues.

2. Each support request should include the student's
ID and a clear description of the issue.

3. The support team reviews requests and provides
updates according to the nature of the issue.

4. Students should retain their support ticket ID
for future communication.

SECTION 5: ATTENDANCE POLICY

1. Students are expected to follow the attendance
requirements communicated by their academic programme.

2. Attendance exceptions must be discussed with
the academic office.

3. The specific attendance percentage requirement
is not defined in this demonstration handbook.
